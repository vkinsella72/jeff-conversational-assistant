# Korean-Style Beef Mince over Rice (Quick Bulgogi Bowl)

**Category:** beef
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 10 min **Cook:** 15 min **Total:** 25 min

## Quick description

The 25-minute weeknight bulgogi. Beef mince cooked in a sweet-savory Korean-style sauce (soy + brown sugar + ginger + garlic + sesame), served over rice with a fried egg on top. **The fried egg is non-negotiable — the runny yolk is the sauce for the rice.**

## Ingredients

- 500 g beef mince
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger (or 1 tsp dried)
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp brown sugar (or honey)
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp dried chilli flakes (optional, for heat)
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 4 eggs (one per person)
- 2 spring onions, sliced (white and green parts separated)
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted
- Steamed rice, to serve
- Quick-Pickled Red Onions (sides/04) or sliced cucumber, to serve

## Method

1. **Make the sauce.** In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, brown sugar, rice vinegar, sesame oil, ginger, garlic, and chilli flakes (if using). Set aside.
2. **Cook the onion.** Heat the vegetable oil in a large frying pan or wok over medium-high heat. Add the onion (white parts of the spring onion too, if using) and cook for 4-5 minutes until softened and starting to colour.
3. **Brown the mince.** Add the beef mince, breaking it up with a wooden spoon. Cook for 6-8 minutes until well-browned.
4. **Add the sauce.** Pour the sauce over the mince. Stir to coat. Let it bubble for 2-3 minutes until the sauce has thickened and is glossy.
5. **Fry the eggs.** While the mince finishes, heat a small non-stick pan over medium-high. Crack in the eggs, season with salt and pepper, and fry for 2-3 minutes until the whites are set but the yolks are still runny.
6. **Serve.** Pile rice into bowls, top with the beef mince, then a fried egg, then sliced spring onion greens, then sesame seeds. Quick-pickled red onions or sliced cucumber on the side.

## Notes / variations

- **The fried egg is the move.** Don't skip it. The runny yolk + the sweet-savory mince + the rice is the whole point.
- **Gochujang (Korean chilli paste) optional:** if you happen to have it, a tablespoon stirred into the sauce at the end takes this from "good" to "lush" with a real Korean heat. Sainsbury's and Tesco both stock it.
- **Want it more substantial?** Add a bag of frozen stir-fry veg with the onion, or some frozen peas in the last 2 minutes.
- **No brown sugar?** Honey works. White sugar + a touch of molasses also works.
- **Leftover mince:** brilliant in a wrap the next day, or stuffed into a brioche bun with slaw.

## Pairs with

- Steamed jasmine rice
- Kimchi (if you've got it in the fridge)
- A cold Korean beer or jasmine tea
- Quick-Pickled Red Onions (sides/04) on top
