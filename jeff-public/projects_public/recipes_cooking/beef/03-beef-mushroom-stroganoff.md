# Beef & Mushroom Stroganoff (Sour Cream, Mustard, Egg Noodles)

**Category:** beef
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 10 min **Cook:** 25 min **Total:** 35 min

## Quick description

The classic British-Russian comfort dish, weeknight-edition. Strips of steak (or mince) in a creamy mushroom, mustard, and sour cream sauce, served over egg noodles. **The trick is cooking the beef fast and hot so it stays tender, and adding the sour cream off the heat so it doesn't split.**

## Ingredients

- 500 g beef steak (sirloin or rump, trimmed), cut into thin strips
- 250 g chestnut mushrooms, sliced
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 200 ml sour cream
- 100 ml beef stock
- 2 tbsp brandy or red wine (optional, but lush)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp butter
- Salt and black pepper
- 300 g egg noodles (or fusilli in a pinch)
- Fresh parsley or dried parsley, to finish

## Method

1. **Cook the noodles.** Bring a large pan of salted water to the boil. Cook the noodles according to the packet, drain, and set aside.
2. **Brown the beef.** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large frying pan over high heat. Pat the beef strips dry, season with salt and pepper. Sear the beef in batches (don't crowd the pan) for **1-2 minutes per side** until just browned. Transfer to a plate.
3. **Sauté the veg.** Reduce the heat to medium. Add the remaining oil and the butter to the same pan. Add the onion, cook for 4-5 minutes until soft. Add the mushrooms, cook for 5 minutes until they've released their liquid and it's evaporated. Add the garlic and smoked paprika, cook for 1 minute.
4. **Deglaze.** Pour in the brandy (if using) and let it bubble for 30 seconds. Add the beef stock and Dijon mustard, stir to combine, and let it bubble for 2-3 minutes until slightly reduced.
5. **Finish the sauce.** Remove the pan from the heat. Stir in the sour cream (off the heat is important — boiling sour cream splits). Taste and adjust seasoning.
6. **Combine.** Return the beef (and any juices) to the pan. Stir to coat in the sauce and warm through for 1-2 minutes.
7. **Serve** over the egg noodles, scattered with parsley.

## Notes / variations

- **No steak?** Use 500 g beef mince, browned and broken up. Different texture, still lush, more economical.
- **No brandy/wine?** A splash of red wine vinegar or just extra stock works.
- **Sour cream splitting:** if the sauce looks grainy, it's boiled. Whisk a tablespoon of cold water in vigorously — usually rescues it.
- **Want it more "Russian"?** Serve with a dollop of soured cream on top and a sprinkle of dill (dried is fine).
- **Mushroom haters (weirdo):** Leave them out, add a second onion and a handful of frozen peas.

## Pairs with

- Steamed green beans or broccoli
- A glass of red wine (or whatever brandy you didn't use)
- Crusty bread for mopping
- The next day's leftovers make a great pie filling (top with puff pastry, 220°C for 20 min)
