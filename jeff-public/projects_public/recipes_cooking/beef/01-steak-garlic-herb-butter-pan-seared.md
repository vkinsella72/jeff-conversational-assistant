# Steak with Garlic-Herb Butter, Pan-Seared

**Category:** beef
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 2
**Prep:** 5 min (+ 30 min resting) **Cook:** 10 min **Total:** 45 min

## Quick description

The "got a nice steak and want to do it justice" recipe. Pan-seared with garlic-herb butter basted over the top, finished with a rest that lets the juices redistribute. **The trick is bringing the steak to room temperature before cooking, and not moving it for 4 minutes per side. The crust is the point.**

## Ingredients

- 2 steaks (ribeye, sirloin, or rump — around 200-250 g each, 2-2.5 cm thick)
- Salt and black pepper
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil (for the pan)
- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tsp dried thyme or dried rosemary
- 1 tbsp olive oil (for the butter)

### For the garlic-herb butter

- 50 g butter, softened
- 1 garlic clove, crushed
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley (or 1 tsp dried)
- 1 tsp dried thyme or rosemary
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. **Make the herb butter first.** Mash the softened butter with the crushed garlic, herbs, salt, and pepper. Spoon onto a piece of cling film, roll into a log, and pop in the fridge to firm up. (Can be made days ahead.)
2. **Bring the steaks to room temperature.** Take them out of the fridge at least **30 minutes** before cooking. Cold steaks don't cook evenly — the outside overcooks before the middle is right.
3. **Season aggressively.** Pat the steaks dry. Season both sides with plenty of salt and a few grinds of pepper.
4. **Heat the pan.** Get a heavy-based frying pan (cast iron is ideal) smoking hot. Add the vegetable oil — it should shimmer.
5. **Sear.** Lay the steaks in the pan away from you (so any splatter goes away from you). **Don't move them for 4 minutes.** When the underside has a deep brown crust, flip.
6. **Butter baste.** Reduce heat to medium. Add the 3 tbsp butter, crushed garlic, and herbs to the pan. Tilt the pan so the butter pools, and use a spoon to baste the butter over the steaks continuously for **2-3 minutes** (for medium-rare). For medium-well, add another minute per side after the baste.
7. **Rest.** Transfer the steaks to a warm plate, top with a slice of the herb butter, and let them rest for **5-7 minutes** tented loosely with foil. Don't skip this — the juices need to redistribute, otherwise they all run out when you cut.
8. **Serve.** Slice against the grain, spoon any resting juices (and pan butter if you can rescue it) over the top.

## Notes / variations

- **Doneness guide (after the 4-min first sear, flip and baste for the times below):** Rare 2 min total basting, Medium-rare 3 min, Medium 4 min, Well-done 5+ min (but really, please don't).
- **No thermometer?** Touch test: rare = soft like the fleshy bit at the base of your thumb, medium = springy, well-done = firm. Learn it once, use it forever.
- **The pan butter is the sauce.** Don't wash it down the sink. Spoon it over the steak or use it to dress vegetables.
- **No dried herbs?** Just use butter + garlic. Still lush.

## Pairs with

- A peppery green salad or steamed greens
- A glass of something red and serious
- The leftover herb butter on toast the next morning
