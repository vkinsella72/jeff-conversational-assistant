# Mexican Picadillo (Beef Mince, Tomato, Olives, Raisins)

**Category:** beef
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 10 min **Cook:** 25 min **Total:** 35 min

## Quick description

The "pantry surprise" recipe that makes mince taste like a proper Mexican plate. Beef mince cooked with onion, garlic, tomatoes, olives, raisins, and warm spices. The raisins melt into the sauce giving it depth, the olives give it salt and bite. Spoon over rice, stuff into tacos, or pile onto nachos. **Freezer mince, tinned tomatoes, store-cupboard spices — the brief, delivered.**

## Ingredients

- 500 g beef mince
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 red pepper, diced
- 1 x 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 50 g raisins (or sultanas)
- 50 g pitted green olives, roughly chopped
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper
- Lime wedges, to serve
- Fresh coriander or dried oregano, to finish (dried is fine)

## Method

1. **Heat the oil** in a large frying pan or saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the onion and cook for 4-5 minutes until softening.
2. **Add the garlic and pepper.** Cook for another 2-3 minutes until the pepper is starting to soften.
3. **Brown the mince.** Add the beef mince, breaking it up with a wooden spoon. Cook for 6-8 minutes until well-browned (not grey — browned bits = flavour).
4. **Add the spices.** Stir in the cumin, coriander, smoked paprika, and cinnamon. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. **Add the wet stuff.** Tip in the chopped tomatoes, tomato purée, and 100 ml water. Add the raisins and olives. Season with salt and pepper.
6. **Simmer.** Reduce the heat and let it bubble gently for **15-20 minutes** until the sauce has thickened and the mince is rich and glossy. Taste and adjust — you might want a pinch more salt, or a squeeze of lime to brighten it.
7. **Serve** over rice, in tacos, on nachos, or stuffed into baked potatoes. Scatter with coriander or oregano, serve with lime wedges.

## Notes / variations

- **The raisins are not optional.** They melt into the sauce and add the depth that makes this "lush" rather than "just mince." Trust the recipe.
- **Want it hotter?** Add 1 chopped fresh chilli with the garlic, or ½ tsp chilli powder with the other spices.
- **Want it more substantial?** Add a tin of drained red kidney beans or black beans in the last 10 minutes of simmering.
- **Leftover moves:** Stuff into wraps with cheese and salad. Pile onto nachos with grated cheese under the grill. Use as the base for a chilli (add a tin of kidney beans and a splash of beer).
- **Freezer-friendly:** Yes — cools, freezes in portions, defrosts in the microwave for 4-5 min.
