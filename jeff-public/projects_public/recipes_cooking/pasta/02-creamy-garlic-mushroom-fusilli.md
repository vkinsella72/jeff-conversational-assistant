# Creamy Garlic Mushroom Fusilli

**Category:** pasta
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 5 min **Cook:** 20 min **Total:** 25 min

## Quick description

The 25-minute comfort pasta. Lots of mushrooms, lots of garlic, a splash of white wine, and cream. **The trick is getting the mushrooms properly browned — don't crowd the pan, let them sit, and you'll get the caramelisation that makes this "lush."**

## Ingredients

- 400 g chestnut mushrooms, sliced
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 5 garlic cloves, crushed
- 300 g fusilli pasta
- 200 ml double cream
- 100 ml white wine (or chicken stock with a squeeze of lemon)
- 50 g butter
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 50 g grated parmesan (plus more to serve)
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- Salt and black pepper
- Fresh parsley or dried parsley, to finish

## Method

1. **Cook the pasta.** Bring a large pan of salted water to the boil. Cook the fusilli according to the packet until al dente. Reserve 100 ml of the pasta water before draining.
2. **Brown the mushrooms.** While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil and half the butter in a large frying pan over high heat. Add the mushrooms in a single layer (work in batches if needed — **don't crowd them**). Cook for 4-5 minutes without moving, until deeply browned. Flip and brown the other side. Transfer to a plate.
3. **Soften the aromatics.** Reduce heat to medium. Add the remaining butter to the pan. Add the onion, cook for 4-5 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and thyme, cook for 1 minute.
4. **Deglaze.** Pour in the white wine, scraping up the brown bits on the bottom of the pan. Let it bubble for 2 minutes until reduced by half.
5. **Add the cream.** Pour in the cream, bring to a gentle simmer, and let it thicken for 2-3 minutes.
6. **Combine.** Return the mushrooms to the pan, add the drained pasta and the parmesan. Toss to coat, adding a splash of the reserved pasta water if the sauce is too thick. Season with salt and pepper.
7. **Serve** with more parmesan and a scatter of parsley.

## Notes / variations

- **Mushroom browning is the entire dish.** Wet mushrooms in a crowded pan steam — they go grey and rubbery. Dry mushrooms in a hot, roomy pan with patience = golden, caramelised, deeply savoury. **Don't skip the batch-cooking step.**
- **No white wine?** Chicken or vegetable stock with a squeeze of lemon at the end works.
- **Want it richer?** Stir in another 50 g butter at the end. The French call it "monter au beurre" — finishing with cold butter. Makes everything glossy and silky.
- **Add protein:** Sliced chicken breast (pan-fry first), cooked prawns, or crispy bacon pieces stirred in at the end.
- **Vegan version:** Skip the cream and parmesan, use 200 ml coconut milk + 2 tbsp nutritional yeast. Different vibe, still works.

## Pairs with

- Garlic bread (or just toast with butter)
- A peppery rocket salad with lemon
- A glass of the same white wine you cooked with
- The leftovers reheat well with a splash of milk to loosen the sauce
