# Lemon-Garlic Prawn Fusilli (or Chicken)

**Category:** pasta
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 5 min **Cook:** 20 min **Total:** 25 min

## Quick description

The 25-minute "I want something summery and a bit special" pasta. Prawns (or chicken) in a lemon-garlic-chilli-white-wine-cream sauce, tossed with fusilli. **The lemon zest at the end is the move — that's where the brightness lives.**

## Ingredients

- 300 g fusilli pasta
- 400 g raw king prawns (or 2 chicken breasts, sliced)
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 5 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 red chilli, finely chopped (or 1 tsp dried chilli flakes)
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 100 ml white wine
- 200 ml double cream
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and black pepper
- Fresh parsley or dried parsley, to finish
- Parmesan (optional)

## Method

1. **Cook the pasta.** Bring a large pan of salted water to the boil. Cook the fusilli until al dente. Reserve 100 ml pasta water before draining.
2. **Sauté the aromatics.** Heat the olive oil and butter in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 4-5 minutes until soft. Add the garlic, chilli, and oregano, cook for 1 minute.
3. **Cook the protein.** Add the prawns (or chicken) to the pan. If using prawns, cook for 2-3 minutes until pink and just cooked. If using chicken, cook for 5-6 minutes until golden and cooked through.
4. **Deglaze.** Pour in the white wine and lemon juice. Let it bubble for 2-3 minutes until reduced by half.
5. **Add the cream.** Reduce the heat to low. Stir in the cream and let it warm through for 2-3 minutes. Don't boil.
6. **Combine.** Add the drained pasta and the lemon zest to the pan. Toss to coat, adding pasta water if the sauce is too thick.
7. **Serve** with a scatter of parsley and a grind of black pepper.

## Notes / variations

- **The lemon zest is non-negotiable.** The juice gives acidity, the zest gives fragrance. They're doing different jobs. Microplane or fine grater for the zest.
- **Frozen prawns are fine** — defrost in cold water for 5 minutes, pat dry, then cook. Don't cook from frozen, they'll water out the sauce.
- **Chicken version:** Use 2 chicken breasts, sliced into strips, browned and cooked through before the aromatics. Add a splash more cream to compensate for no prawn juices.
- **Add spinach:** 200 g frozen spinach, defrosted and squeezed dry, stirred in at the end. Adds colour and bulk.
- **Want it lighter?** Use crème fraîche instead of cream. Tangier, less rich.

## Pairs with

- Crusty bread (or just a spoon)
- A green salad with a sharp dressing
- A glass of cold white wine (whatever you didn't cook with)
- A wedge of lemon on the side for the lemon obsessives
