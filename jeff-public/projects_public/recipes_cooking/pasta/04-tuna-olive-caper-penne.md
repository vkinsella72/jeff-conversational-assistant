# Tuna, Olive & Caper Penne (Pasta alla Puttanesca Light)

**Category:** pasta
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 5 min **Cook:** 20 min **Total:** 25 min

## Quick description

The pantry pasta. Tinned tuna, tinned tomatoes, capers, olives, garlic, chilli. 25 minutes, almost entirely from the cupboard, tastes like it took hours. **The capers and anchovies (if using) are doing the deep, salty, savoury work — don't skip them.**

## Ingredients

- 300 g penne pasta
- 2 x 185 g tins tuna in olive oil, drained
- 1 x 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 50 g pitted black olives, roughly chopped
- 2 tbsp capers, drained
- 4 anchovy fillets in oil, chopped (optional, but lush)
- 1 tsp dried chilli flakes
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper
- Fresh parsley or dried parsley, to finish
- Parmesan (controversial in puttanesca, but you can if you want)

## Method

1. **Cook the pasta.** Bring a large pan of salted water to the boil. Cook the penne according to the packet until al dente. Reserve 100 ml pasta water before draining.
2. **Build the sauce.** While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 4-5 minutes until soft. Add the garlic, anchovies (if using), chilli flakes, and oregano. Cook for 2 minutes, mashing the anchovies with a wooden spoon until they dissolve.
3. **Add tomatoes, olives, capers.** Stir in the tinned tomatoes, olives, and capers. Simmer for 8-10 minutes until the sauce has thickened.
4. **Add the tuna.** Flake the drained tuna into the sauce. Stir gently to combine. Taste and adjust — you probably won't need salt (capers, olives, anchovies are all salty).
5. **Combine.** Add the drained pasta to the sauce, toss to coat, adding a splash of the reserved pasta water if needed.
6. **Serve** with a scatter of parsley.

## Notes / variations

- **The anchovies melt into the sauce.** They don't taste fishy — they add a deep, savoury, umami thing. If you skip them, add an extra tablespoon of capers or a splash of soy sauce.
- **No fresh parsley?** Dried parsley in the sauce (½ tsp) is fine.
- **Spicier?** More chilli flakes. Lots more.
- **Parmesan on puttanesca:** traditionalists say no. I say yes if you want it. The fight has been going on for 50 years and neither side has won.
- **Tinned tuna > fresh tuna here** because the cooking in the sauce gives it a different texture — softer, more integrated. Don't substitute fresh.
