# Spicy Sausage & Tomato Rigatoni (Sausage Ragù)

**Category:** pasta
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 10 min **Cook:** 30 min **Total:** 40 min

## Quick description

The "slow Sunday-feeling" pasta that's actually weeknight-quick. Hot Italian sausages (or spiced pork sausages) in a rich tomato-wine sauce, tossed with rigatoni or penne. **The rigatoni tubes catch the sauce in a way fusilli doesn't — the rigatoni is doing work here.**

## Ingredients

- 6 hot Italian sausages (or 4 regular pork sausages + 1 tsp chilli flakes)
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 carrots, finely diced
- 2 celery sticks, finely diced
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 x 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 150 ml red wine
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil (or 1 tsp dried mixed Italian herbs)
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper
- 300 g rigatoni or penne
- Parmesan, to serve
- Fresh basil or dried oregano, to finish

## Method

1. **Brown the sausages.** Heat the olive oil in a large saucepan or deep frying pan over medium-high heat. Squeeze the sausage meat out of the casings (or use whole and break up later). Brown for 6-8 minutes, breaking up with a wooden spoon, until well-caramelised. Transfer to a plate.
2. **Soffritto base.** In the same pan, add the onion, carrots, and celery. Cook for 8-10 minutes until softened and starting to colour. (This is the "soffritto" — the Italian flavour base. Don't rush it.)
3. **Add garlic and spices.** Stir in the garlic, oregano, basil, and bay leaf. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. **Deglaze.** Pour in the red wine, let it bubble for 2-3 minutes until reduced by half.
5. **Add tomatoes.** Stir in the tinned tomatoes and tomato purée. Season with salt and pepper.
6. **Simmer.** Return the sausage to the pan. Reduce heat and let it bubble gently, uncovered, for **15-20 minutes** until the sauce has thickened and the flavours have melded. Taste and adjust.
7. **Cook the pasta.** Meanwhile, bring a large pan of salted water to the boil. Cook the rigatoni according to the packet until al dente. Drain, reserving 100 ml pasta water.
8. **Combine.** Tip the drained pasta into the sauce, toss to coat, adding a splash of pasta water if it needs loosening. Fish out the bay leaf.
9. **Serve** with grated parmesan and a scatter of basil or oregano.

## Notes / variations

- **The soffritto is doing 70% of the work.** That's the onion + carrot + celery base, cooked slowly until sweet and soft. Don't shortcut this.
- **Want it saucier?** Add a splash of stock or water at the end.
- **No red wine?** A splash of red wine vinegar with extra stock, or just chicken stock.
- **Rigatoni > penne here** because the tubes catch the chunky sauce. But penne works fine in a pinch.
- **Make-ahead:** The sauce is even better the next day. Make it ahead, reheat, cook fresh pasta when ready.

## Pairs with

- Crusty bread for mopping
- A simple green salad
- A glass of red wine (or whatever you didn't use in the sauce)
- A wedge of hard cheese for the table
