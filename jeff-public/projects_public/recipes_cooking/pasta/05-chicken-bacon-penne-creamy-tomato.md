# Chicken, Bacon & Penne in Creamy Tomato Sauce

**Category:** pasta
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 5 min **Cook:** 25 min **Total:** 30 min

## Quick description

The "pasta bake without actually baking it" pasta. Chicken, bacon, mushrooms, and penne in a creamy tomato sauce. Quicker than a pasta bake, just as comforting, and you don't have to wait for the oven. **The bacon is non-negotiable — it gives the base its smoky, savoury backbone.**

## Ingredients

- 300 g penne pasta
- 4 chicken thigh fillets (boneless, skinless), diced
- 200 g smoked bacon lardons (or streaky bacon, chopped)
- 250 g chestnut mushrooms, sliced
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 x 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 200 ml double cream
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper
- 50 g grated parmesan
- Fresh parsley or dried parsley, to finish

## Method

1. **Cook the pasta.** Bring a large pan of salted water to the boil. Cook the penne until al dente. Reserve 100 ml pasta water before draining.
2. **Crisp the bacon.** Heat the olive oil in a large deep frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the bacon and cook for 5-6 minutes until crispy and the fat has rendered. Transfer to a plate, leaving the fat in the pan.
3. **Brown the chicken.** Add the diced chicken to the pan (in batches if needed). Cook for 5-6 minutes until golden and cooked through. Transfer to the plate with the bacon.
4. **Sauté the veg.** In the same pan, add the onion and cook for 4-5 minutes until soft. Add the mushrooms, cook for 5 minutes until browned. Add the garlic, oregano, and basil, cook for 1 minute.
5. **Add tomatoes.** Pour in the tinned tomatoes. Simmer for 5-8 minutes until thickened.
6. **Add cream.** Reduce the heat to low. Stir in the cream and let it warm through for 2-3 minutes. Don't boil.
7. **Combine.** Return the chicken and bacon (and any juices) to the pan. Add the drained pasta and the parmesan. Toss to coat, adding pasta water if the sauce is too thick.
8. **Serve** with more parmesan and a scatter of parsley.

## Notes / variations

- **The bacon fat is the cooking medium.** Don't drain it. That's where the smoky flavour comes from.
- **No chicken thighs?** Use 2 chicken breasts, diced. Thighs are juicier but breasts work.
- **Want it spicy?** Add 1 tsp chilli flakes with the garlic, or use chorizo instead of bacon.
- **Make it a pasta bake:** Tip into a baking dish, top with more parmesan and mozzarella, bake at 200°C for 15-20 min until golden and bubbling. The full restaurant-feel version.
- **Vegan version:** Skip chicken and bacon, use mushrooms + smoked tofu + coconut cream. Different, but works.
