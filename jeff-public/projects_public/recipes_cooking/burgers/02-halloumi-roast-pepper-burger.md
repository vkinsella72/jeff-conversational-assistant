# Halloumi & Roast-Pepper Burger (Vegetarian, Griddled)

**Category:** burgers
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 2
**Prep:** 5 min **Cook:** 10 min **Total:** 15 min

## Quick description

The 15-minute vegetarian burger that doesn't taste vegetarian. Halloumi griddled until golden, with jarred roast peppers, lettuce, tomato, and a quick lemon-garlic yoghurt sauce. **Halloumi on the griddle is the move — it gets these gorgeous golden char lines that make it look and taste like a proper burger patty.**

## Ingredients

### For the burgers

- 250 g halloumi, cut into 2 thick slices (about 1.5 cm)
- 2 brioche buns
- 4 jarred roast pepper halves (from a jar, drained)
- A few lettuce leaves
- 1 tomato, sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil (for the griddle)
- Butter, for the buns

### For the lemon-garlic yoghurt sauce

- 4 tbsp Greek yoghurt (or natural yoghurt)
- 1 garlic clove, crushed
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper
- 1 tsp dried mint or dried oregano (optional)

## Method

1. **Make the sauce.** Whisk all the sauce ingredients together in a small bowl. Set aside.
2. **Heat the griddle.** Get the griddle (or a heavy-based frying pan) smoking hot. Brush with olive oil.
3. **Griddle the halloumi.** Pat the halloumi slices dry with kitchen paper. Lay them on the griddle. **Don't move them for 2-3 minutes** until deep golden char lines form. Flip and cook the other side for 2 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
4. **Griddle the peppers.** Lay the drained roast pepper halves on the griddle for 1-2 minutes per side until charred. Transfer to the plate.
5. **Toast the buns.** Butter the cut sides of the buns and toast them cut-side down on the griddle for 30-60 seconds.
6. **Assemble.** Sauce the bottom bun with the yoghurt sauce. Layer lettuce, tomato, the halloumi slice (cut in half if it doesn't fit), the roast peppers, more sauce, top bun.

## Notes / variations

- **Halloumi on the griddle needs to be dry** — wet halloumi sticks and steams. Pat it dry with kitchen paper before it goes on.
- **The yoghurt sauce is doing a lot of work.** It cuts through the salty halloumi and ties everything together. Don't skip it.
- **No roast peppers?** Sliced fresh red pepper, griddled with the halloumi, also works. Less sweet, still good.
- **Want it more substantial?** Add sliced avocado, or some griddled aubergine, or a few rocket leaves for peppery bite.
- **Halloumi sub:** No halloumi? Use a thick slice of feta (but it crumbles — different vibe) or a portobello mushroom, griddled.
- **Make it vegan:** Use a vegan halloumi (most supermarkets do one) and a plant-based yoghurt.

## Pairs with

- Sweet potato fries or a simple green salad
- A cold beer or a glass of rosé
- Extra yoghurt sauce for dipping the fries
- A watermelon and feta salad on the side (if you're being properly Mediterranean)
