# Garlic-Butter Frozen Broccoli with Parmesan

**Category:** sides
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 2 min **Cook:** 8 min **Total:** 10 min

## Quick description

The 10-minute side that stops frozen broccoli being boring. Pan-fried in garlic butter with a generous grating of parmesan. **The trick is not covering the pan — you want the broccoli to char at the edges, not steam.**

## Ingredients

- 500 g frozen broccoli florets
- 3 tbsp butter
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 30 g grated parmesan
- 1 tsp dried chilli flakes (optional)
- Zest of ½ lemon (optional)
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. **Heat the butter and oil** in a large frying pan over medium-high heat until the butter is foaming.
2. **Add the garlic and chilli flakes** (if using), cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. **Add the frozen broccoli** straight from the freezer. Spread in a single layer. **Don't cover the pan** — you want char, not steam.
4. **Cook for 6-8 minutes**, turning every couple of minutes, until the broccoli is bright green, tender, and charred at the edges.
5. **Off the heat**, scatter with the parmesan, lemon zest (if using), and a generous grind of black pepper. Toss to coat.
6. **Serve immediately.**

## Notes / variations

- **Don't cover the pan** — covering steams the broccoli and you lose the char. If you have a lid, just don't use it.
- **Frozen is fine** — the florets are flash-frozen at peak, so they're often better than the sad "fresh" broccoli that sits in the fridge.
- **Add nuts:** Toasted flaked almonds or pine nuts scattered on top add texture and richness.
- **Add bacon:** Crispy bacon pieces stirred through at the end.
- **Vegan:** Skip the parmesan, use all olive oil. Or use nutritional yeast (1-2 tbsp) for the cheesy depth.
- **Other frozen veg this works with:** Cauliflower, Brussels sprouts (halved), green beans, asparagus.

## Pairs with

- Literally everything. Steak, chicken, fish, pasta, rice. It's the "what veg should I do?" answer.
