# Mexican-Style Rice (Restaurant-Style, One-Pan)

**Category:** sides
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 5 min **Cook:** 25 min **Total:** 30 min

## Quick description

The proper Mexican restaurant-style rice (the one that comes as a side with your burrito). Long-grain rice, toasted in oil until golden, then simmered in tomato stock with garlic and onion. **The trick is toasting the rice in oil first — that's what gives it that nutty, separate-grain texture, not the sticky rice you get if you just boil it.**

## Ingredients

- 300 g long-grain rice (basmati works, or use long-grain white if you have it)
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 x 200 g tin chopped tomatoes (or 3 fresh tomatoes, blended)
- 500 ml hot chicken or vegetable stock
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 bay leaf (optional)
- Fresh coriander or dried parsley, to finish
- Lime wedges, to serve

## Method

1. **Rinse the rice.** Put the rice in a sieve and rinse under cold water until the water runs clear. Drain well.
2. **Toast the rice.** Heat the vegetable oil in a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the rice and stir constantly for 3-4 minutes until the grains are golden and starting to turn translucent at the edges. **Don't walk away** — it can burn quickly.
3. **Soften the aromatics.** Add the onion to the rice and cook for 3-4 minutes until soft. Add the garlic, cumin, and oregano, cook for 1 minute.
4. **Add the tomato and stock.** Tip in the tinned tomatoes and the hot stock. Add the salt, pepper, and bay leaf if using. Stir to combine.
5. **Simmer.** Bring to the boil, then reduce to the lowest heat. Cover with a tight-fitting lid. Cook for **15 minutes** without lifting the lid.
6. **Rest.** Remove from the heat. Let it sit, lid on, for another 5 minutes.
7. **Finish.** Remove the lid, fluff with a fork. The rice should be fluffy, with separate grains and a slight orange-red colour. Stir through the chopped coriander or parsley. Serve with lime wedges.

## Notes / variations

- **Toasting the rice is the entire game.** Skipping this step gives you sticky, mushy rice. Toasting gives you nutty, separate grains that soak up the tomato stock.
- **The lid stays on for the whole 15 min cook + 5 min rest.** Lifting the lid releases the steam that cooks the rice evenly.
- **Want it more tomato-ey?** Add an extra tablespoon of tomato purée when you add the tinned tomatoes.
- **Add veg:** A cup of frozen peas and diced carrots stirred in at the end.
- **Make-ahead:** Holds well in a warm oven (covered) for 30 min. Reheat with a splash of water.
- **Freezer-friendly:** Cooked rice freezes well for up to 3 months. Defrost in the microwave with a splash of water.
