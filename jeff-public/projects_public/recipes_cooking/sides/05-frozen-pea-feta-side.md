# Frozen-Pea, Feta & Lemon Side

**Category:** sides
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 2 min **Cook:** 5 min **Total:** 7 min

## Quick description

The 7-minute side that punches above its weight. Frozen peas cooked just until bright green, then dressed with olive oil, lemon, feta, and mint. **The trick is NOT overcooking the peas — they need to keep their bite and their colour. Bright green, not khaki.**

## Ingredients

- 500 g frozen peas
- 100 g feta, crumbled
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tsp dried mint (or 2 tbsp fresh, chopped)
- Salt and black pepper
- 1 tbsp pine nuts or flaked almonds, toasted (optional)

## Method

1. **Cook the peas.** Bring a saucepan of water to the boil. Tip in the frozen peas, bring back to the boil, and cook for exactly **2 minutes**. Drain immediately.
2. **Dress.** Tip the hot peas into a serving bowl. Add the olive oil, lemon zest and juice, mint, salt, and pepper. Toss to coat. (The heat of the peas helps the lemon and oil emulsify into a light dressing.)
3. **Top.** Scatter the crumbled feta and toasted nuts (if using) over the top. Serve warm or at room temperature.

## Notes / variations

- **Don't overcook the peas.** 2 minutes is the sweet spot. Any longer and they go khaki and mushy. Bright green = right.
- **The lemon juice hits the hot peas** and the acid "cooks" them slightly, brightening the flavour.
- **No dried mint?** Basil or parsley (dried) work. Or skip — feta + lemon is enough.
- **Add garlic:** A crushed garlic clove sautéed in the olive oil before dressing the peas. Adds depth.
- **Add bacon:** Crispy bacon lardons stirred through. The salty-bacony-pea combination is classic for a reason.
- **Serve warm or cold:** Both work. Cold is great as a salad the next day.
