# Honey-Glazed Roasted Carrots

**Category:** sides
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 5 min **Cook:** 35 min **Total:** 40 min

## Quick description

The proper side that goes with anything from Sunday roast to midweek chicken. Carrots roasted with honey, butter, garlic, and thyme until tender and caramelised at the edges. **The honey + butter combo is doing the work — they caramelise in the oven and create a sticky, glossy glaze.**

## Ingredients

- 600 g carrots (baby carrots or regular carrots cut into batons)
- 3 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp honey
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper
- Fresh parsley or dried parsley, to finish

## Method

1. **Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).**
2. **Prep the carrots.** If using baby carrots, just halve them lengthwise. If using regular carrots, peel and cut into batons about the thickness of your thumb.
3. **Make the glaze.** Melt the butter in a small saucepan (or microwave). Whisk in the honey, garlic, thyme, salt, and pepper.
4. **Toss and arrange.** Tip the carrots onto a large baking tray. Drizzle with the olive oil and pour over the honey-butter glaze. Toss to coat. Spread in a single layer.
5. **Roast for 30-35 minutes**, turning halfway through, until the carrots are tender and the edges are caramelised and sticky.
6. **Finish.** Scatter with parsley. Serve hot.

## Notes / variations

- **The honey burns easily** — if the edges are going too dark before the carrots are tender, turn the heat down to 180°C and cover loosely with foil for the remaining time.
- **Want them more savoury?** Skip the honey, use 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar + 1 tbsp olive oil instead. Different vibe, also good.
- **Add spices:** 1 tsp ground cumin or smoked paprika for a different direction.
- **Use baby carrots whole** — they look more impressive and the presentation is "lush."
- **The pan juices are GOLDEN** — spoon them over the carrots or over whatever protein you're serving.
- **Make-ahead:** Roast earlier in the day, reheat in a 180°C oven for 10 min.
