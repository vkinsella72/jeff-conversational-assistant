# Quick-Pickled Red Onions (10-min, last 2 weeks)

**Category:** sides
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 1 jar (about 200 g)
**Prep:** 5 min (+ 30 min pickling) **Cook:** 5 min **Total:** 40 min

## Quick description

The "transforms any sandwich/burger/taco" condiment. Red onions pickled in vinegar, sugar, and salt — ready in 30 minutes, keep for 2 weeks in the fridge. **The trick is slicing the onions as thin as you can — paper-thin if you have a mandoline, otherwise just take your time with a sharp knife.**

## Ingredients

- 2 red onions
- 200 ml red wine vinegar (or white wine vinegar, or apple cider vinegar)
- 200 ml water
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black peppercorns
- 1 bay leaf (optional)
- 1 tsp dried chilli flakes (optional)

## Method

1. **Slice the onions.** Peel the onions. Slice them as thinly as you can — a mandoline is ideal (use the guard!), otherwise a sharp knife and patience. You want rings or half-rings, not chunks.
2. **Pack into a jar.** Tip the sliced onions into a clean jar (a 500 ml Kilner jar or similar). Pack them in but don't squash.
3. **Make the pickling liquid.** In a small saucepan, combine the vinegar, water, sugar, salt, peppercorns, bay leaf, and chilli flakes (if using). Bring to the boil, stirring until the sugar and salt dissolve.
4. **Pour over the onions.** Pour the hot pickling liquid over the onions, making sure they're fully submerged. Tap the jar on the bench to release any air bubbles.
5. **Cool and pickle.** Let it cool to room temperature (about 30 minutes), then screw the lid on and refrigerate. They're ready to eat in 30 minutes, but better after 2-3 hours, and even better the next day.
6. **Keeps in the fridge for 2-3 weeks.** The onions will gradually become more pickled and the liquid will turn pink — that's normal and beautiful.

## Notes / variations

- **The pickling liquid can be reused** — once you've eaten the onions, top up with fresh sliced onions and they'll pickle in the existing liquid.
- **Add aromatics:** A garlic clove, a few sprigs of thyme (dried is fine), a cinnamon stick, star anise. Experiment.
- **Sweetness:** Adjust the sugar to taste. More sugar = sweeter pickle, less = sharper.
- **Use them on:** Burgers, tacos, salads, sandwiches, grain bowls, hot dogs, avocado toast, eggs, cheese boards. The list is endless.
- **Pink onions:** Don't be alarmed when the onions turn the pickling liquid bright pink. That's the anthocyanins in red onions doing their thing. It's beautiful and edible.

## Pairs with

- Everything. The point of pickled red onions is that they go with everything.
- Specifically: smashed burgers, halloumi burgers, fish tacos, mushroom stroganoff on toast, the Korean beef bowl.
