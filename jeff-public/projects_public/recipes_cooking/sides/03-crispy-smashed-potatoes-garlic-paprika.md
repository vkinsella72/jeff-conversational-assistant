# Crispy Smashed Potatoes with Garlic & Paprika

**Category:** sides
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 5 min **Cook:** 50 min **Total:** 55 min

## Quick description

The 55-minute side that everyone asks about. Baby potatoes boiled until tender, smashed flat, drizzled with garlic-paprika oil, baked until properly crispy. **The smashing is the trick — you want crinkly, uneven surfaces for maximum crunch.**

## Ingredients

- 1 kg baby potatoes (or new potatoes)
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and black pepper
- Fresh parsley or dried parsley, to finish

## Method

1. **Boil the potatoes.** Put the potatoes in a large saucepan, cover with cold salted water. Bring to the boil, then simmer for **20-25 minutes** until tender (a knife slides in easily). Drain well.
2. **Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan).**
3. **Make the oil.** In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, garlic, smoked paprika, oregano, salt, and pepper.
4. **Smash the potatoes.** Line a large baking tray with baking paper. Place the drained potatoes on the tray, leaving space between them. Using the bottom of a glass or a potato masher, press down firmly on each potato until it smashes to about 1 cm thick. (They'll crack and split — that's the look you want.)
5. **Drizzle and season.** Drizzle the garlic-paprika oil generously over the smashed potatoes, making sure each one gets some garlic. Season with extra salt and pepper.
6. **Bake for 25-30 minutes** until the edges are deeply golden and crispy. Don't open the oven in the first 20 minutes.
7. **Finish.** Scatter with parsley. Serve hot.

## Notes / variations

- **The smashing is the move.** Cracked, crinkly surfaces = more crispy bits. Smooth-flat surfaces = sad, less crispy potatoes.
- **Use baby potatoes** (small ones) — they smash more easily and have better potato-to-crispy-edge ratio.
- **The hot oven is essential.** 220°C minimum. Lower than that and they go soft rather than crispy.
- **Want them cheesy?** Sprinkle grated parmesan over the top in the last 5 minutes of baking.
- **Add a dip:** Sour cream + chives, garlic aioli, or even just ketchup.
- **Make-ahead:** Boil and smash the potatoes ahead, then bake just before serving.
