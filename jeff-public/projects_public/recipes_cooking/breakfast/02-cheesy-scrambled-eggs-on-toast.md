# Cheesy Scrambled Eggs on Toast (Done Right)

**Category:** breakfast
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 1-2
**Prep:** 2 min **Cook:** 8 min **Total:** 10 min

## Quick description

The 10-minute breakfast that ruins all other scrambled eggs. Soft, creamy curds, proper cheese, on hot buttered toast. **The trick is low heat, constant stirring, and pulling the pan off the heat 30 seconds before you think they're done — they keep cooking from residual heat.**

## Ingredients

- 4 eggs
- 2 tbsp milk or single cream
- Salt and black pepper
- 20 g butter
- 30 g grated cheese (cheddar, gruyère, or whatever you've got)
- 2 slices of bread (or 1 barm, halved)
- Butter, for the toast
- Chilli flakes or hot sauce (optional)

## Method

1. **Beat the eggs.** Crack the eggs into a bowl. Add the milk, a pinch of salt, and a few grinds of pepper. Whisk with a fork until completely combined and slightly frothy.
2. **Heat the pan low.** Put the butter in a non-stick frying pan over **low heat**. Let it melt and foam but not brown. (Brown butter = pan too hot, eggs will be tough.)
3. **Cook the eggs slowly.** Pour in the eggs. Using a silicone spatula or wooden spoon, stir constantly in small circles, pushing the curds from the outside into the middle. The eggs should form soft, wet curds.
4. **Pull them off early.** When the eggs look about 80% done (still slightly wet on top, with soft curds forming), remove the pan from the heat. **Keep stirring** for 30 seconds — the residual heat will finish them to creamy perfection. (If you wait until they look 100% done in the pan, they'll be overcooked and dry on the plate.)
5. **Add the cheese.** Stir in the grated cheese so it just melts. Taste and adjust seasoning.
6. **Toast the bread.** While the eggs cook, toast the bread and butter it generously.
7. **Serve immediately** on the hot toast, with a scatter of chilli flakes if you like heat.

## Notes / variations

- **Low and slow is the entire game.** High heat = rubbery eggs. Low heat = soft, creamy curds. It's a 5-minute difference in time and a world of difference in texture.
- **Crème fraîche instead of milk** in the eggs makes them even creamier. Or skip the dairy entirely for a denser, more eggy result.
- **The cheese goes in off the heat** so it melts gently without making the eggs stringy.
- **Add-ons:** A handful of chopped chives (if you've got them — dried is fine), some smoked salmon, or a few slices of avocado on top.
- **Make it a full English:** Add bacon, sausage, mushrooms, tomatoes. But then it's not really scrambled eggs any more, is it.

## Pairs with

- A glass of orange juice or a hot drink
- A sliced tomato with a pinch of salt
- Hot sauce, brown sauce, HP, or tomato ketchup (pick to taste)
