# Sausage, Egg & Cheese Breakfast Barm

**Category:** breakfast
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 2
**Prep:** 2 min **Cook:** 12 min **Total:** 15 min

## Quick description

The 15-minute hangover cure. Sausage, egg, cheese, and any sauce you like, in a soft barm. **The trick is cooking the sausage so it gets a proper sear and a bit of char — that's the difference between a sad barm and a proper one.**

## Ingredients

- 4 pork sausages (good ones — proper butchers if you can)
- 2 barms (or 2 large baps)
- 2 eggs
- 4 slices of cheese (cheddar, American-style, or whatever you've got)
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- Butter, for the barms
- Brown sauce, ketchup, HP, or hot sauce (your call)
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. **Cook the sausages.** Heat the vegetable oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the sausages and cook for 10-12 minutes, turning regularly, until well-browned and cooked through. (You can also do this in the oven at 200°C for 20-25 min if you're doing a big batch.)
2. **Halve and butter the barms.** While the sausages cook, slice the barms in half and butter the cut sides. (Don't toast them — the sausage heat will warm them through in the barm.)
3. **Fry the eggs.** In the same pan as the sausages (or a separate one), crack in the eggs. Season and fry to your liking — runny yolk is the move for the barm.
4. **Assemble.** On the bottom half of each barm, lay a slice of cheese (it'll melt from the sausage heat). Pile on the sausages, then the fried egg, then another slice of cheese on top. Sauce generously. Top with the barm lid. Press down gently.
5. **Eat immediately**, while the cheese is still melting and the yolk is still runny.

## Notes / variations

- **Quality of sausage matters more than any other ingredient here.** Get the best you can — proper pork sausages with a high meat content. The cheap ones are fine for casseroles but they make a sad barm.
- **The cheese inside the barm melts from residual heat.** If you're impatient, pop the open barm under the grill for 30 seconds to melt the cheese. If you want it messier and more indulgent.
- **No barms?** Burger buns or any soft roll works. Just don't use crusty bread — it fights the soft texture of the sausage and egg.
- **Add-ons:** Fried bacon, hash browns, mushrooms, black pudding. Or just keep it simple.
- **Sauce debate:** Brown sauce and HP are both traditional; ketchup and hot sauce are valid modern alternatives. Pick to taste.
- **The fried-egg-runny-yolk-as-sauce rule** is non-negotiable. If you don't like runny yolks, this isn't the barm for you.

- Hash browns or tinned beans on the side if you're properly hungry
