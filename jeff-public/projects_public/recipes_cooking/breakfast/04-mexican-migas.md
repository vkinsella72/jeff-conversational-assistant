# Mexican Migas (Tortilla, Egg, Cheese, Salsa Skillet)

**Category:** breakfast
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 2
**Prep:** 5 min **Cook:** 15 min **Total:** 20 min

## Quick description

The Mexican "use up stale tortillas" breakfast that became a classic. Crispy tortilla strips scrambled with eggs, cheese, onion, and a quick salsa. **The crispy tortilla strips are doing the work — they're like the croutons of this dish.**

## Ingredients

- 4 small tortillas (or 2 large), cut into 2 cm strips
- 4 eggs
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 x 200 g tin chopped tomatoes (or 2 fresh tomatoes, diced)
- 1 red pepper, finely diced (or ½ green pepper)
- 50 g grated cheese (cheddar, Monterey Jack, or feta)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp dried chilli flakes (optional)
- Salt and black pepper
- Sour cream or natural yoghurt, to serve (optional)
- Hot sauce, to serve (optional)
- Avocado, sliced, to serve (optional)

## Method

1. **Make a quick salsa.** Tip the tinned tomatoes into a small pan with half the onion, the garlic, smoked paprika, and cumin. Simmer for 8-10 minutes until thickened. Season.
2. **Crisp the tortillas.** Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the tortilla strips in a single layer and cook for 3-4 minutes, flipping once, until crispy and golden. Transfer to a plate.
3. **Sauté the veg.** In the same pan, add the remaining oil. Add the rest of the onion and the red pepper. Cook for 4-5 minutes until softening.
4. **Add eggs.** Beat the eggs in a bowl with a pinch of salt and pepper. Pour into the pan with the veg. Stir gently, like scrambled eggs, until almost set.
5. **Add the tortilla strips.** Return the crispy tortilla strips to the pan. Toss to combine. The strips will soften slightly from the egg — that's the move.
6. **Finish.** Stir in the grated cheese so it just melts. Spoon onto plates, top with the salsa, sour cream, avocado, and hot sauce if using.

## Notes / variations

- **Stale tortillas are better than fresh** — they crisp up without going chewy. Day-old wraps or tortillas are perfect.
- **The salsa is doing 30% of the work.** Don't skip it (or use a shop-bought one if you must).
- **Add chorizo:** 100 g cooking chorizo, browned and crumbled, stirred in with the veg. Adds smoky depth and bulk.
- **No tortillas?** Stale wraps work. Or even torn-up sliced bread, crisped in the pan first.
- **Vegan version:** Skip eggs, use crumbled firm tofu seasoned with the same spices. Different texture, similar vibe.
- **The texture is the point.** You want crispy bits AND soft bits. Don't over-stir once the tortillas are in.
