# Huevos Rancheros (Frozen-Style)

**Category:** breakfast
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 2
**Prep:** 5 min **Cook:** 15 min **Total:** 20 min

## Quick description

The 20-minute brunch that uses everything in the freezer and the cupboard. Tortillas, refried beans (tinned), eggs, cheese, and a quick tomato salsa. **Frozen-style because we're using tinned beans and frozen veg where possible — no fresh chilli or fresh coriander required.**

## Ingredients

### For the quick salsa

- 1 x 400 g tin chopped tomatoes (or 4 ripe tomatoes, diced)
- ½ red onion, finely chopped
- 1 garlic clove, crushed
- 1 tsp red wine vinegar (or lime juice if you've got one)
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- Salt and pepper

### For the rest

- 1 x 400 g tin refried beans (or 1 x 400 g tin black beans, drained and roughly mashed)
- 4 small tortillas (flour or corn)
- 4 eggs
- 100 g grated cheese (cheddar, Monterey Jack, or a mix)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 x 400 g tin refried beans
- 1 avocado, sliced (optional)
- Sour cream or natural yoghurt, to serve (optional)
- Hot sauce, to serve (optional)

## Method

1. **Make the salsa.** Tip the tinned tomatoes into a small saucepan with the red onion, garlic, vinegar, and cumin. Simmer for 8-10 minutes until thickened. Season with salt and pepper. (Or just chop everything fresh and skip the cooking — different vibe, also good.)
2. **Warm the beans.** Tip the refried beans into a small saucepan with a splash of water. Warm through over medium heat, stirring, for 3-4 minutes. Season with salt, pepper, and a pinch of smoked paprika.
3. **Fry the tortillas.** Heat a splash of oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Fry each tortilla for 30 seconds per side until just charred and flexible. Stack on a plate and cover to keep warm.
4. **Fry the eggs.** In the same pan, crack in the eggs. Season with salt and pepper. Fry to your liking (runny yolk is the move).
5. **Assemble.** On each plate, lay a tortilla, spread with warm beans, top with a fried egg, spoon over the salsa, scatter with grated cheese. Repeat the layers if you want to be fancy. Top with avocado slices and a dollop of sour cream if using.

## Notes / variations

- **The runny yolk is the sauce.** Don't overcook the eggs — you want the yolk to break over the beans and tortilla.
- **No refried beans?** Black beans (tinned, drained, roughly mashed) work. Add a pinch of cumin and smoked paprika to season.
- **No tortillas?** Toast makes a fine base. Or use wraps if that's all you've got.
- **Want it meaty?** Add some chorizo or bacon — cooked first, then beans warmed in the chorizo fat.
- **Make it a breakfast barm:** Fold everything into a warm barm or roll. Less photogenic, more portable.
- **Salsa timing:** The cooked tomato salsa is more "brunch-y." For a fresher salsa, just chop tomatoes + onion + garlic + lime + salt and skip the cooking.
