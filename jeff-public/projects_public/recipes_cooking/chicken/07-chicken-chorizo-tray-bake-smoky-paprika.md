# Chicken & Chorizo Tray Bake with Smoky Paprika Potatoes

**Category:** chicken
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 10 min **Cook:** 50 min **Total:** 1 hour

## Quick description

The tray bake that tastes like a Sunday roast that's been on a Spanish holiday. Chicken thighs + chorizo + new potatoes, all roasted together with smoked paprika + garlic + olive oil. The chorizo renders its paprika-red oil into everything. **One tray, one oven, the chorizo does the work.**

## Ingredients

- 4-6 chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on
- 400 g new potatoes (or baby potatoes), halved
- 200 g cooking chorizo, sliced into 1 cm coins
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 1 red pepper, sliced into chunks
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp smoked paprika (hot or sweet, your call)
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and black pepper
- Lemon wedges, to serve
- Fresh parsley or dried oregano, to finish (dried is fine)

## Method

1. **Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).**
2. **Prep the tray.** In a large roasting tray, toss the potatoes, red onion, and red pepper with the olive oil, smoked paprika, oregano, garlic, salt, and pepper. Spread in one layer.
3. **Add the chicken and chorizo.** Make space in the tray for the chicken thighs (skin-up). Tuck the chorizo slices in and around everything.
4. **Roast for 45-50 minutes.** At the halfway point (25 min), baste the chicken with the pan juices and give everything a quick toss. The chicken should be deeply golden, the potatoes crisp at the edges, the chorizo crisped and rendered its red oil.
5. **Finish.** Squeeze over the lemon wedges, scatter with parsley or extra oregano, serve straight from the tray.

## Notes / variations

- **The chorizo is doing two jobs:** flavouring the potatoes as it renders AND crisping up in the dry heat. Don't substitute it for anything else — there's no equivalent.
- **Cooking chorizo vs cured chorizo:** You want the soft, cooking chorizo (the kind that comes in a sausage shape and you slice it), not the cured Spanish chorizo you'd put on a board. Both are great, but different jobs.
- **No new potatoes?** Any waxy potato works. Floury potatoes (Maris Piper etc.) go fluffier — also good but less "tray bake-y."
- **Want more veg?** Throw in some cherry tomatoes in the last 15 minutes, or some whole garlic cloves that you can squeeze out and spread on bread.
- **Want it spicier?** Add a second teaspoon of smoked paprika (hot this time) or a pinch of chilli flakes.

## Pairs with

- A green salad with a sharp vinaigrette (cuts through the chorizo richness)
- Crusty bread to mop the tray
- A cold Spanish lager or a glass of red
- The next day's leftovers in a wrap with salad
