# Honey-Mustard-Garlic Pan Chicken

**Category:** chicken
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-21
**Created:** 2026-07-21
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 2-4
**Prep:** 10 min **Cook:** 20 min **Total:** 30 min

## Quick description

Weeknight classic. Skin-down thighs in a hot pan, golden and crisp, then a glaze of honey + Dijon + cream or water + crushed garlic, finished with lemon. Pan sauce, not a stew. Serve over rice or with crusty bread to mop the sauce. The sweet-sharp-creamy combo doesn't need fresh herbs to taste like a million quid.

## Ingredients

- 4-6 chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on
- Salt and black pepper
- 1 tbsp oil (olive or vegetable)
- 4-6 garlic cloves, crushed or finely chopped
- 3 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 100 ml double cream OR 100 ml water/stock
- Juice of half a lemon
- 1 tsp dried thyme or dried oregano (optional, body of flavour)
- Rice, mashed potato, or crusty bread to serve

## Method

1. Pat the chicken thighs **very dry** with kitchen paper. Season both sides generously with salt and pepper. Dry skin = crispy skin.
2. Heat the oil in a wide heavy pan (cast iron or heavy-bottomed) over medium-high heat until shimmering.
3. Place the thighs **skin-down** in the pan. Don't move them. Cook 8-10 minutes until the skin is deep golden and releases easily from the pan.
4. Flip the thighs, cook 3-4 minutes on the other side to render some fat and get colour.
5. Push the thighs to one side. Lower heat to medium. Add the garlic to the cleared space, stir for 30 seconds until fragrant (don't let it burn).
6. Add the honey, Dijon, and cream (or water). Stir to combine, scraping up any brown bits from the pan — that's the flavour.
7. Nestle the thighs back in the sauce. Simmer 5-7 minutes, spooning the sauce over the top, until the chicken is cooked through (juices run clear, internal temp 75°C).
8. Squeeze the lemon juice over, give a final stir. Taste the sauce — add more salt, honey, or lemon to balance.
9. Serve over rice / mash / with bread. Spoon the sauce over everything.

## Notes / variations

- **No cream?** Use coconut milk, crème fraîche, or just water/stock. Cream is richest, water is lightest.
- **No Dijon?** English mustard works, or 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard.
- **Want it spicy?** Add a pinch of chilli flakes with the garlic.
- **Dried herbs:** thyme or oregano. If you skip them, add a bit more lemon at the end to keep the sauce bright.

## Pairs with

- Steamed rice or creamy mashed potato
- Steamed green beans or a peppery rocket salad
- Cold beer or a glass of something white and crisp
