# Sticky Soy-Ginger-Honey Tray Bake

**Category:** chicken
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-21
**Created:** 2026-07-21
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 2-4
**Prep:** 10 min **Cook:** 40 min **Total:** 50 min

## Quick description

Lowest effort for highest reward. Thighs on a tray with root veg, drenched in a soy + honey + ginger + garlic + sesame oil glaze. Goes in the oven at 200°C and you forget about it for 40 min. Glaze caramelises, veg soaks up the drippings. Umami bomb that doesn't need fresh herbs — the soy-honey-ginger combo is its own flavour world. Makes the kitchen smell incredible. **Jeff's pick when the pantry is full and you want to put your feet up.**

## Ingredients

- 4-6 chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on
- 500 g veg, cut into 2-3 cm chunks (any combo: potatoes, sweet potato, carrots, peppers, red onion, courgette, aubergine — all work)
- 4 tbsp honey
- 3 tbsp soy sauce (dark or light)
- 1 tbsp sesame oil (or vegetable oil if you haven't got it)
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger (or 1 tsp dried ginger)
- 4 garlic cloves, grated or crushed
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar or white wine vinegar (optional, brightens it)
- 1 tsp dried chilli flakes (optional, heat)
- Sesame seeds, to finish (optional)
- Spring onions, sliced, to finish (optional)
- Salt and black pepper
- Oil for the tray

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Get a large roasting tray out — you want everything in one layer, not stacked.
2. In a small bowl, whisk together the honey, soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger, garlic, vinegar, and chilli flakes (if using). This is your glaze.
3. Cut the veg into chunks. Toss in a bowl with a glug of oil, salt, and pepper.
4. Tip the veg onto the tray and spread in one layer. Drizzle about **a third** of the glaze over the veg, toss to coat.
5. Pat the chicken thighs dry. Season with salt and pepper. Place them **skin-up** on top of the veg.
6. Brush or spoon the **remaining two-thirds** of the glaze generously over the chicken thighs. Use it all.
7. Roast 35-40 minutes. At the halfway point (20 min), baste the thighs with the pan juices. The glaze should be deeply caramelised, the veg tender, the chicken skin burnished and sticky.
8. Optional: for extra char, finish under the grill for 2-3 minutes. **Watch it like a hawk** — honey burns fast.
9. Rest 5 minutes. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and spring onions if you've got them. Serve straight from the tray, family-style.

## Notes / variations

- **Veg swaps:** nearly anything works. Hard veg (potato, carrot, sweet potato) needs the full 40 min. Soft veg (courgette, pepper, tomato) can go in halfway through.
- **No fresh ginger?** Use 1 tsp dried ginger, or 1 tsp ground ginger, or skip and lean on the garlic.
- **Want it saucier?** Double the glaze, drizzle over rice or noodles when serving.
- **Want it darker / more umami?** Add 1 tbsp dark soy or 1 tsp Chinese five-spice.

## Pairs with

- Steamed jasmine or basmati rice (soaks up the glaze)
- Or rice noodles, or just crusty bread
- A cold lager, or jasmine tea if it's a weeknight
