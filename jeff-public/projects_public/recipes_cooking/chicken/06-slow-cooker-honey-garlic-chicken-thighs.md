# Slow-Cooker Honey-Garlic Chicken Thighs

**Category:** chicken
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 10 min **Cook:** 4 hours (low) or 2 hours (high) **Total:** 4 hours 10 min

## Quick description

The winter slow-cooker rule in action. Chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on, swimming in a honey-soy-garlic-ginger sauce, cooked low and slow until they're falling-apart tender and the sauce has reduced to a sticky glaze. Hands-off cooking at its finest. **Spoon over rice, scatter with spring onion, done.**

## Ingredients

- 6-8 chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on
- 4 tbsp honey
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar (or white wine vinegar)
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 6 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger (or 1 tsp dried)
- 1 tsp dried chilli flakes (optional, for a bit of heat)
- 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with 2 tbsp cold water (to thicken the sauce at the end)
- Spring onion, sliced, to finish
- Sesame seeds, to finish
- Steamed rice, to serve

## Method

1. **Whisk the sauce.** In the slow-cooker bowl, whisk together the honey, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and chilli flakes (if using).
2. **Add the chicken.** Pat the chicken thighs dry. Place them skin-up in the sauce — they should be mostly submerged, with the skin above the sauce line if possible (this keeps the skin from going rubbery).
3. **Slow-cook.** Cover and cook on **LOW for 4 hours** or **HIGH for 2 hours**, until the chicken is cooked through (internal temp 75°C) and tender.
4. **Optional skin-crisp step.** For proper restaurant-quality skin, transfer the cooked thighs to a baking tray and place under a hot grill/broiler for 3-5 minutes until the skin crisps and chars slightly. **Watch it** — the honey burns fast.
5. **Thicken the sauce.** Ladle about 200 ml of the cooking liquid into a small saucepan. Bring to a simmer and whisk in the cornflour slurry. Simmer for 2-3 minutes until thickened to a glossy glaze. Taste and adjust — if it's too sweet, a splash more soy; too salty, a splash more honey.
6. **Serve.** Pile rice into bowls, top with the chicken thighs, spoon the thickened glaze over the top, scatter with spring onion and sesame seeds.

## Notes / variations

- **The skin-crisp step is the difference between "fine" and "lush."** Without it, slow-cooker chicken skin is rubbery. With it, you've got something that looks and tastes like a proper restaurant plate.
- **No slow-cooker?** A covered casserole dish in a 160°C oven for 2 hours works. Same liquid, same temperature check, same crisp-finish at the end.
- **Boneless thighs?** Yes — reduce LOW to 3 hours, HIGH to 1.5 hours.
- **Leftovers?** Strip the meat, mix with the sauce, and you've got the next day's lunch rice bowl sorted. Or stuff into a brioche bun with slaw.
- **Want it saucier?** Double the sauce ingredients. The chicken will not complain.

## Pairs with

- Steamed jasmine rice (soaks up the glaze)
- Stir-fried greens or frozen broccoli with sesame
- Pickled cucumber on the side
- Cold Japanese lager or jasmine tea
