# Lemon-Garlic Butter Chicken Breasts, Pan-Fried

**Category:** chicken
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 2
**Prep:** 5 min **Cook:** 15 min **Total:** 20 min

## Quick description

The summer-pan-fry rule in action. Two chicken breasts, butterflied so they cook fast and even, seared in a hot pan, then finished in a lemon-garlic-butter pan sauce. 20 minutes, no oven, looks like a restaurant plate. **The move is butterflying the breasts — a thick breast won't cook through in a pan without drying out, but a butterflied one does in 6-7 min.**

## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breasts (around 180-200 g each)
- Salt and black pepper
- 2 tbsp plain flour (for a light dredge)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 tbsp butter
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 lemon (zest + juice)
- 1 tbsp capers (optional, but lush)
- Fresh parsley or dried oregano, to finish (dried is fine)
- Steamed greens or rice, to serve

## Method

1. **Butterfly the breasts.** Place each breast flat on a board, hand flat on top, and slice horizontally through the middle (parallel to the board) to make two thinner steaks of equal thickness. You should end up with 4 thin pieces. **Don't skip this** — thick breasts overcook on the outside before the middle is done.
2. **Season and dredge.** Pat the butterflied breasts dry with kitchen paper. Season both sides generously with salt and pepper. Lightly dredge in flour, shaking off the excess.
3. **Sear.** Heat the olive oil in a heavy-based frying pan over medium-high heat until shimmering. Lay the breasts in the pan (in batches if needed — don't crowd them). Cook for **3-4 minutes per side** until deeply golden and cooked through. Transfer to a warm plate, cover loosely.
4. **Pan sauce.** Reduce the heat to medium. Add the butter to the same pan. Once melted and foaming, add the crushed garlic and capers (if using). Cook for 30 seconds until fragrant — don't let the garlic brown.
5. **Finish the sauce.** Squeeze in the juice of the lemon and add the zest. Let it bubble for 30 seconds, scraping up any brown bits on the bottom of the pan. Taste — it should be sharp, buttery, salty.
6. **Serve.** Spoon the pan sauce over the chicken, scatter with parsley or dried oregano, serve with rice or steamed greens.

## Notes / variations

- **No butterflying?** Pound the breasts between two sheets of cling film to an even 1.5 cm thickness instead. Same result, slightly more effort.
- **No capers?** A few chopped green olives work, or just skip — the lemon-butter is the star.
- **Dried herbs in the sauce?** ½ tsp dried oregano or thyme is a good sub for the parsley.
- **Want it creamy?** Add 2 tbsp double cream to the sauce at step 5. Different vibe, also lush.
- **What to do with the pan bits?** Don't wash them — they're the entire flavour of the sauce. That's why we deglaze with the lemon juice.
