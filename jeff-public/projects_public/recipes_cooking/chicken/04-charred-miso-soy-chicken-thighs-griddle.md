# Charred Miso-Soy Chicken Thighs on the Griddle

**Category:** chicken
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 2
**Prep:** 10 min (+ 10 min marinating) **Cook:** 12 min **Total:** 30 min

## Quick description

The griddle is a banger and this is the recipe that proves it. Chicken thighs, skin-on, marinated in white miso + soy + honey + ginger + garlic, then seared hard on the griddle so the skin blisters and the fat renders down into the griddle lines. Sticky rice + sesame greens to soak up the pan juices. Restaurant-quality, weeknight effort. **The move is hot griddle, don't move the chicken for 4-5 min per side, let the skin do the work.**

## Ingredients

- 4 chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on
- 1.5 tbsp white miso paste (Sainsbury's, Tesco, Lidl all do it — about £2-3 a tub, lasts months in the fridge)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger (or 1 tsp dried)
- 3 garlic cloves, grated or crushed
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil (for the griddle)
- Sesame seeds, to finish
- Spring onion, sliced, to finish (optional)

### For the rice

- 200 g jasmine or basmati rice
- 300 ml water
- 1 tsp salt

### For the sesame greens

- 250 g frozen broccoli (or frozen peas, or a mix)
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- Sesame seeds, to finish

## Method

1. **Marinate the chicken.** In a bowl big enough to hold the thighs, whisk together the miso, soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, sesame oil, ginger, and garlic. Add the chicken thighs and toss to coat. Leave to marinate for **at least 10 minutes** while you get the rice on (longer is better — 30 min if you can).
2. **Cook the rice.** Rinse the rice in cold water until it runs clear. Tip into a saucepan with 300 ml water and a pinch of salt. Bring to the boil, cover with a tight lid, reduce to the lowest heat, and cook for **12 minutes**. Remove from the heat and let it sit, lid on, for another 5 minutes. Don't lift the lid.
3. **Heat the griddle.** Put the griddle over two gas rings on a medium-high heat for 3-4 minutes until properly hot. You want the griddle hot enough that a drop of water dances on it. Brush the griddle with the vegetable oil.
4. **Griddle the chicken.** Lift the chicken thighs out of the marinade, letting the excess drip off. Place them **skin-down** on the griddle. **Don't move them for 4-5 minutes** — the skin needs to sear hard and release naturally. If it sticks, it's not ready to flip. When the skin is deeply golden and lifts easily, flip and cook the other side for 4-5 minutes until the meat is cooked through (internal temp 75°C, or the juices run clear when you cut at the bone).
5. **Rest the chicken.** Transfer the thighs to a plate, cover loosely with foil, and let them rest for 5 minutes.
6. **Sesame greens.** While the chicken rests, get a pan or wok smoking hot. Add the sesame oil, then the frozen broccoli (straight from the freezer — no need to defrost). Stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until bright green and just tender. Add the soy sauce, honey, and grated garlic, toss to coat, cook for another minute. Scatter with sesame seeds.
7. **Serve.** Fluff the rice with a fork. Pile onto plates, top with the chicken thighs, spoon any juices from the resting plate over the top, and finish with sesame seeds and sliced spring onion if you've got it.

## Notes / variations

- **Miso is the new move.** It's not exotic — it's a £2-3 tub in the world food aisle that lasts months. The depth it adds is "lush" in a way plain soy can't reach. Once you've got it, the marinade works on salmon, pork chops, even firm tofu.
- **No griddle?** Use a heavy-based frying pan or griddle pan on the highest heat. Won't give you the same lines but the sear is the point, not the lines.
- **Boneless thighs?** Yes — reduce the cook time to 3-4 min per side.
- **Chicken breasts?** Don't use this method. They're too lean for the griddle sear; they'll go dry. Use the Lemon-Garlic Butter Chicken Breasts recipe instead.
- **Rice shortcut?** Microwave rice pouches are fine. The sesame greens are 5 min either way.
- **Make it a rice bowl.** Slice the rested chicken, pile on the rice, top with greens and any extra sauce. The leftovers are better the next day.

## Pairs with

- The leftover marinade, brushed on during the last minute of griddling (it's already cooked through contact with the chicken, so safe)
- A cold Japanese lager or a dry white wine
- Pickled cucumber on the side (Quick-Pickled Red Onions works too)
