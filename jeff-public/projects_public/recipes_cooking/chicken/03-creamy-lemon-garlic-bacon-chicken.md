# Creamy Lemon-Garlic-Bacon Chicken

**Category:** chicken
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-21
**Created:** 2026-07-21
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 2-4
**Prep:** 10 min **Cook:** 30 min **Total:** 40 min

## Quick description

A bit of effort, restaurant-tier. Pan-seared thighs finished in a creamy lemon-garlic-bacon sauce. The bacon + cream + lemon combo doesn't need fresh herbs — the bacon fat does the heavy lifting. This is the "impress yourself on a Tuesday" option. Serve over pasta, mashed potato, or rice.

## Ingredients

- 4-6 chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on (or skinless if you prefer)
- 150 g streaky bacon or pancetta, chopped into lardons
- 1 tbsp oil (only if your bacon is lean)
- 6-8 garlic cloves, sliced or crushed (be generous)
- 150 ml dry white wine (or chicken stock, or a splash of vermouth)
- 200 ml double cream
- Zest of 1 lemon + juice of half (taste before adding the full juice)
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- Salt and black pepper
- 1 tsp dried parsley (optional, finish)
- Pasta, mashed potato, or rice to serve

## Method

1. Pat the chicken thighs **very dry**. Season generously with salt and pepper.
2. Heat a wide heavy pan (cast iron or deep frying pan) over medium-high heat. Add the bacon and cook 4-5 minutes until crispy and the fat has rendered. Remove the bacon with a slotted spoon, leave the fat in the pan.
3. Place the chicken thighs **skin-down** in the bacon fat. Don't crowd the pan — work in batches if needed. Cook 8-10 minutes until deeply golden and the skin releases easily.
4. Flip the thighs, cook 3-4 minutes on the other side. Remove the thighs, set aside on a plate. (They'll finish in the sauce.)
5. Lower the heat to medium. Add the garlic to the pan, stir for 30 seconds until fragrant (don't burn it).
6. Pour in the wine (or stock). Scrape up every brown bit from the bottom of the pan with a wooden spoon — that's the flavour. Let it bubble down by about half, 2-3 minutes.
7. Add the cream, lemon zest, lemon juice, and Dijon. Stir to combine. Simmer 2 minutes to thicken slightly.
8. Return the chicken thighs and the crispy bacon to the pan, skin-side up so it stays crisp. Spoon some sauce over the thighs (not the skin). Simmer gently 5-7 minutes until the chicken is cooked through (75°C internal, juices run clear).
9. Taste the sauce. Add more salt, lemon, or a grind of black pepper. If it's too thick, add a splash of water. Too thin, simmer another minute.
10. Serve over pasta, mash, or rice. Spoon the sauce over. Sprinkle with dried parsley if using.

## Notes / variations

- **No wine?** Use chicken stock plus 1 tsp white wine vinegar. Or just use more cream and a bit more lemon.
- **No cream?** Crème fraîche works. Or coconut milk for a different vibe (less rich, more fragrant).
- **Skinless thighs?** Use a bit more oil to start, and you'll miss the crispy skin but the sauce compensates.
- **Want it heartier?** Throw in a handful of spinach at the end, or some sliced mushrooms with the garlic.
- **Lemon caution:** lemon juice can curdle the cream if you add it too early or on too high heat. Add it with the cream, off the boil if possible, then simmer gently.

## Pairs with

- Tagliatelle, pappardelle, or any ribbon pasta
- Creamy mashed potato
- Steamed greens: asparagus, tenderstem broccoli, or peas
- Crisp white wine (Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc) or a pale ale
