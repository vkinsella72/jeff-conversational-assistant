# Crispy Air-Fryer Chicken Tenders with Honey-Mustard Dip

**Category:** chicken
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 2 (or 4 as a starter)
**Prep:** 10 min **Cook:** 12 min **Total:** 22 min

## Quick description

The air-fryer recipe that actually delivers on the air-fryer promise. Chicken tenders (strips of breast), crusted in panko + parmesan + smoked paprika, air-fried until properly golden and crispy. No deep-fryer, no soggy breadcrumbs, no guilt. **The honey-mustard dip is non-negotiable.**

## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breasts (around 350 g total)
- 100 g panko breadcrumbs
- 30 g grated parmesan
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 50 g plain flour
- Olive oil spray (or a few tbsp vegetable oil)

### For the honey-mustard dip

- 3 tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard (or English mustard, milder)
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. **Cut the chicken.** Slice each breast horizontally into 2-3 thin steaks, then cut each steak into 2-3 cm wide strips. You should end up with about 12-16 tenders.
2. **Set up the dredging station.** Three shallow bowls: (1) flour seasoned with salt and pepper, (2) beaten eggs, (3) panko mixed with the parmesan, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and a pinch of salt.
3. **Coat the tenders.** Working one at a time: dredge in flour (shake off excess), dip in egg (let excess drip off), then press firmly into the panko mixture to coat all sides. Place on a plate.
4. **Preheat the air-fryer** to 200°C for 3 minutes.
5. **Air-fry.** Spray the basket with oil. Lay the tenders in a single layer (work in batches — don't crowd). Spray the tops with oil. Cook for **6 minutes**, flip, spray again, cook for another **4-6 minutes** until deeply golden and crispy and the chicken is cooked through (internal temp 75°C).
6. **Make the dip.** While the tenders cook, whisk together the mayonnaise, mustard, honey, and lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper.
7. **Serve immediately**, with the dip on the side.

## Notes / variations

- **The panko + parmesan combo is the trick.** Panko alone is crispy but bland; parmesan alone burns. Together, they give you the golden, cheesy, crispy crust that makes these restaurant-quality.
- **The oil spray matters.** Without it, the panko doesn't crisp properly. A refillable oil mister is a £10 investment that makes air-fryer cooking ten times better.
- **No air-fryer?** Oven at 220°C for 18-20 minutes, flipping halfway. Won't be quite as crispy but still good.
- **Want them spicier?** Add ½ tsp cayenne to the panko mix, or a pinch of chilli flakes.
- **Make-ahead?** Coat the tenders in the panko up to 4 hours ahead, keep covered in the fridge, air-fry when needed. The coating stays crispier this way than if you reheat cooked tenders.

## Pairs with

- Sweet potato fries (air-fry at the same time)
- A simple green salad
- A cold beer
- The dip works on everything — chips, burgers, fish
