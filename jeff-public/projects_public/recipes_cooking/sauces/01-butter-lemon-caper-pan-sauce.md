# Pan Sauce: Butter, Lemon, Capers (For Chicken or Fish)

**Category:** sauces
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 2-4 (sauce for 2-4 portions)
**Prep:** 2 min **Cook:** 5 min **Total:** 7 min

## Quick description

The 5-minute "fancy restaurant" sauce that elevates plain chicken or fish to something special. Butter, lemon, capers, a splash of white wine, the brown bits from the pan. **The trick is making it in the same pan you cooked the protein in — the fond (brown bits) is the flavour.**

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 small shallot (or ½ small onion), finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 tbsp capers, drained
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 50 ml white wine (or chicken stock with a squeeze of lemon)
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley (or 1 tsp dried)
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. **Use the pan you cooked the chicken or fish in.** Pour off all but 1 tbsp of fat. Put the pan back over medium heat.
2. **Sauté the aromatics.** Add 1 tbsp of the butter. When melted, add the shallot and cook for 2-3 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and capers, cook for 30 seconds.
3. **Deglaze.** Pour in the white wine and lemon juice. Let it bubble for 1-2 minutes, scraping up all the brown bits on the bottom of the pan.
4. **Mount with butter.** Reduce heat to low. Add the remaining 1 tbsp butter and the lemon zest. Swirl the pan (or whisk) until the butter is melted and the sauce is glossy.
5. **Finish.** Stir in the parsley. Taste and adjust seasoning (capers are salty, so you may not need much salt).
6. **Pour over the chicken or fish.** Serve immediately.

## Notes / variations

- **The fond is the flavour.** Don't wash the pan before making the sauce. Those brown bits = restaurant quality.
- **No shallot?** Use the white part of a spring onion, or just skip.
- **No white wine?** Chicken or vegetable stock with an extra squeeze of lemon works.
- **Make it creamy:** Add 2 tbsp double cream at the end for a creamy lemon-caper sauce.
- **Pairs with:** Pan-fried chicken breasts, pan-fried white fish, grilled pork chops, steamed vegetables, plain pasta.
