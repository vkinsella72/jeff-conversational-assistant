# Quick Tomato Sauce (Tinned, Onions, Garlic, Basil)

**Category:** sauces
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4 (about 600 ml)
**Prep:** 5 min **Cook:** 25 min **Total:** 30 min

## Quick description

The 30-minute tomato sauce that beats any jarred sauce. Tinned tomatoes, onion, garlic, olive oil, basil, a pinch of sugar. **The trick is giving it time to simmer — even 20 minutes transforms tinned tomatoes from "tinny" to "sweet and rich."**

## Ingredients

- 1 x 400 g tin chopped tomatoes (or 2 x 400 g for a bigger batch)
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp sugar (to balance the acidity)
- Salt and black pepper
- Fresh basil or dried basil, to finish
- 1 tbsp butter (optional, to finish)

## Method

1. **Soften the onion.** Heat the olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5-6 minutes until soft and translucent.
2. **Add garlic and purée.** Add the garlic and tomato purée, cook for 2 minutes (the purée caramelises and adds depth).
3. **Add tomatoes and herbs.** Tip in the tinned tomatoes, oregano, basil, sugar, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine.
4. **Simmer for 15-20 minutes**, uncovered, until the sauce has thickened. Stir occasionally to stop it catching on the bottom.
5. **Taste and adjust.** You may want more sugar (if too acidic), more salt, or a pinch of dried chilli flakes (for heat).
6. **Finish.** Stir in the butter (if using) for a glossy finish. Scatter with fresh or dried basil.

## Notes / variations

- **The sugar is not optional** — tomatoes are acidic, the sugar balances the acidity. Just 1 tsp makes a difference.
- **The simmer is the move.** 20 minutes minimum. Even better if you can give it 30-40.
- **Want it smoother?** Blend with a stick blender before finishing.
- **Want it meatier?** Add 200 g browned mince, or some cooked sausage meat.
- **Want it more substantial?** Toss with cooked pasta and top with parmesan. Or use as the base for any pasta bake.
- **Freezer-friendly:** Yes — portion into 200 ml containers, freeze for up to 3 months. Defrost in the microwave or overnight in the fridge.
- **Use as a base for:** Pizza (spread on the base), lasagne, bolognese, chicken parm, anything that wants a tomato sauce.
