# 3-Ingredient Teriyaki Glaze

**Category:** sauces
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4 (about 200 ml)
**Prep:** 1 min **Cook:** 5 min **Total:** 6 min

## Quick description

The 6-minute teriyaki glaze that beats any shop-bought bottle. Soy + honey + ginger + garlic, simmered until syrupy. **The trick is letting it reduce until it coats the back of a spoon — too thin and it runs off, too thick and it burns.**

## Ingredients

- 100 ml soy sauce
- 4 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger (or 1 tsp dried)
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar (optional, for brightness)
- 1 tsp sesame oil (optional)
- 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp cold water (to thicken, optional)

## Method

1. **Whisk everything** (except the cornflour slurry) in a small saucepan.
2. **Bring to a simmer** over medium heat, stirring.
3. **Reduce for 3-5 minutes** until the glaze coats the back of a spoon. (If you want it thicker, whisk in the cornflour slurry and simmer for another 1-2 minutes.)
4. **Cool slightly** — it'll thicken more as it cools.
5. **Use immediately** or store in a jar in the fridge for up to 2 weeks.

## Notes / variations

- **The reduction is doing the work.** Too runny = it slides off. Too thick = it burns on the pan. Aim for "coats the back of a spoon and leaves a clear line when you draw a finger through it."
- **Add chilli:** A pinch of dried chilli flakes or a teaspoon of sriracha for heat.
- **Add citrus:** The zest of an orange or a splash of orange juice for a citrus-teriyaki.
- **No fresh ginger?** 1 tsp dried ginger, or ½ tsp ground ginger.
- **Make it a marinade:** Use it as-is to marinate chicken, salmon, or pork for 30 min before cooking.
- **Make it a dipping sauce:** Use it as a dip for grilled chicken, salmon teriyaki burgers, dumplings, or grilled veg.
