# Creamy Garlic Mushroom Sauce (For Steak or Chicken)

**Category:** sauces
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 2-4
**Prep:** 5 min **Cook:** 15 min **Total:** 20 min

## Quick description

The steakhouse-style sauce that makes a basic steak or chicken breast taste like a £30 plate. Sautéed mushrooms, garlic, brandy, cream, dijon, parsley. **The trick is using the pan you seared the protein in — same fond, same flavour base, same restaurant-quality result.**

## Ingredients

- 300 g chestnut mushrooms, sliced
- 1 small onion (or 2 shallots), finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 tbsp brandy or dry white wine (optional)
- 200 ml double cream
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley (or 1 tsp dried)
- Salt and black pepper
- Squeeze of lemon juice (optional, to finish)

## Method

1. **Use the pan you cooked the steak or chicken in.** Pour off all but 1 tbsp of fat. Put the pan back over medium-high heat.
2. **Brown the mushrooms.** Add the olive oil and half the butter. Add the mushrooms in a single layer (work in batches if needed). Cook for 4-5 minutes without moving until deeply browned. Transfer to a plate.
3. **Sauté the aromatics.** Reduce heat to medium. Add the remaining butter. Add the onion, cook for 3-4 minutes until soft. Add the garlic, cook for 1 minute.
4. **Deglaze.** Pour in the brandy (if using) and let it bubble for 30 seconds, scraping up the brown bits on the bottom of the pan.
5. **Add cream and dijon.** Pour in the cream, add the Dijon mustard. Stir to combine. Simmer for 3-4 minutes until the sauce has thickened to a coating consistency.
6. **Return the mushrooms.** Add the browned mushrooms back to the pan. Stir to coat. Warm through for 1-2 minutes.
7. **Finish.** Stir in the parsley, taste and adjust seasoning. A small squeeze of lemon at the end brightens the whole thing.
8. **Pour over the steak or chicken.** Serve immediately.

## Notes / variations

- **The mushrooms need to brown, not steam.** Wet mushrooms in a crowded pan = grey and rubbery. Dry mushrooms in a hot, roomy pan = golden, caramelised, deeply savoury. Don't skip the batch-cooking.
- **The brandy is optional but lush.** It adds a depth that wine doesn't quite reach. Cheap brandy is fine — you're cooking it off anyway.
- **Want it more "pepper sauce"?** Add 1-2 tsp cracked black peppercorns with the cream.
- **Want it more "diane-style"?** Add a splash of Worcestershire sauce with the brandy.
- **Vegan version:** Skip the cream, use 200 ml coconut milk + 1 tbsp nutritional yeast. Different vibe, still works.
- **Make ahead:** The sauce keeps in the fridge for 3 days. Reheat gently — don't boil, the cream will split.

## Pairs with

- Pan-seared steak (the obvious one)
- Pan-fried chicken breasts
- Grilled pork chops
- Steamed vegetables (pour over the top)
- Mashed potatoes
