# Halloumi & Veg Skewers on the Griddle

**Category:** vegetarian
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 15 min **Cook:** 10 min **Total:** 25 min

## Quick description

The griddle recipe that works for vegetarians AND meat-eaters. Halloumi + courgette + red pepper + red onion, threaded onto skewers, griddled until charred, drizzled with lemon and olive oil. **The griddle is doing the work — those char lines on the halloumi and the veg are what make this look like a restaurant plate.**

## Ingredients

- 250 g halloumi, cut into 2 cm cubes
- 2 courgettes, cut into 1.5 cm thick rounds
- 2 red peppers, cut into 2 cm pieces
- 1 red onion, cut into 2 cm wedges
- 8 cherry tomatoes
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt and black pepper
- Fresh parsley or dried parsley, to finish
- Pita breads, to serve (optional)
- Lemon wedges, to serve

## Method

1. **Prep the veg and halloumi.** Pat the halloumi dry with kitchen paper. Cut all the veg to roughly the same size so they cook evenly. If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for 10 minutes (so they don't burn on the griddle).
2. **Marinate.** In a large bowl, toss the halloumi and veg with the olive oil, lemon zest, oregano, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Let it sit for 5-10 minutes.
3. **Thread the skewers.** Thread halloumi, courgette, red pepper, red onion, and cherry tomatoes onto the skewers, alternating for colour. Don't pack too tight — you want the griddle to make contact with everything.
4. **Heat the griddle.** Get the griddle (or a heavy-based griddle pan) smoking hot. Brush lightly with oil.
5. **Cook the skewers.** Lay the skewers on the griddle. Cook for **3-4 minutes per side**, turning to get char lines on all sides, until the halloumi is golden and the veg is just tender with nice char.
6. **Finish.** Squeeze over the lemon juice, scatter with parsley. Serve with pita breads and lemon wedges.

## Notes / variations

- **The halloumi needs to be dry before marinating.** Wet halloumi won't char properly — it steams.
- **The griddle needs to be hot.** Same rule as the chicken thighs and the halloumi burger — high heat = char, low heat = sad steaming.
- **No skewers?** Just griddle everything loose on the griddle or in a griddle pan. Same flavour, less photogenic.
- **Add mushrooms:** Chestnut or portobello mushrooms are great skewered and griddled. Add them to the rotation.
- **Make it a meal:** Serve over rice with a dollop of yoghurt sauce, or in a warm pita with salad.
- **The marinade is doing the seasoning work.** Don't skip the lemon zest and smoked paprika — they're what makes this taste "lush" rather than "just halloumi and veg."
