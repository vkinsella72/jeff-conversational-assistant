# Spinach, Feta & Filo Pie (Spanakopita, Easy Method)

**Category:** vegetarian
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 6
**Prep:** 15 min **Cook:** 45 min **Total:** 1 hour

## Quick description

The "looks impressive, actually easy" pie. Layers of buttered filo pastry, filled with spinach, feta, ricotta, and herbs. **The trick is keeping the filo covered with a damp tea towel while you work — it dries out fast and becomes brittle.**

## Ingredients

### For the filling

- 500 g frozen spinach, defrosted and squeezed dry (very important — squeeze out as much water as possible)
- 250 g feta, crumbled
- 250 g ricotta
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp grated nutmeg
- Salt and black pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil

### For the pastry

- 1 packet filo pastry (about 270 g, 8-10 sheets)
- 100 g butter, melted
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Sesame seeds, to finish (optional)

## Method

1. **Make the filling.** Heat the olive oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 4-5 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and oregano, cook for 1 minute. Let it cool slightly.
2. **Mix the filling.** In a large bowl, combine the squeezed spinach, crumbled feta, ricotta, beaten eggs, nutmeg, the cooled onion mixture, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
3. **Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan).** Brush a rectangular baking dish (about 20x30 cm) with melted butter.
4. **Layer the filo.** Working quickly (and keeping the filo you haven't used yet covered with a damp tea towel):
   - Lay one sheet of filo in the dish, letting the edges hang over. Brush with melted butter.
   - Repeat with 4 more sheets, layering them and brushing each with butter. Let the edges hang over the sides.
   - Spread the spinach-feta filling evenly over the pastry.
   - Fold the overhanging edges back over the filling.
   - Layer the remaining filo sheets on top, brushing each with butter. Trim or fold any excess.
   - Score the top into 6 portions (cutting through the top layers of pastry, not all the way through) — this makes it easier to cut later.
5. **Brush and finish.** Brush the top with the remaining butter. Scatter with sesame seeds if using.
6. **Bake for 35-40 minutes** until the pastry is deeply golden and crisp, and the filling is set.
7. **Rest for 10 minutes** before cutting along the scored lines. Serve warm or at room temperature.

## Notes / variations

- **Frozen spinach is fine** — actually better than fresh for this because it's more concentrated in flavour. **Squeeze it DRY.** Watery filling = soggy pie.
- **The damp tea towel is the entire filo game.** Filo left uncovered for 2 minutes becomes cardboard. Keep it covered, work one sheet at a time.
- **The melted butter is doing the crispy work.** Don't skimp. Every layer needs butter or it won't crisp.
- **Make-ahead:** Assemble the pie, cover, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours before baking. Or freeze the unbaked pie for up to 1 month. Bake from frozen, adding 10-15 min to the cook time.
- **Smaller portions:** Make individual pies in a muffin tin, layering 2-3 sheets of filo per cup, filling, and 2-3 more on top. Adjust the bake time to 20-25 min.
- **Leftover moves:** Cold spanakopita is delicious. Or reheat in a 180°C oven for 10 min to crisp back up.
