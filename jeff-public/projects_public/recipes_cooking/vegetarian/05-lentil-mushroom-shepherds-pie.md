# Lentil & Mushroom Shepherd's Pie (Vegetarian)

**Category:** vegetarian
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4-6
**Prep:** 15 min **Cook:** 45 min **Total:** 1 hour

## Quick description

The proper comfort food, no meat required. Brown lentils and mushrooms in a rich tomato-wine sauce, topped with creamy mash, baked until golden and bubbling. **The trick is the deep, dark flavour base — soy sauce, red wine, tomato purée, dried thyme. They make the lentils taste meaty, not "vegetarian."**

## Ingredients

### For the filling

- 250 g dried green or brown lentils (or 2 x 400 g tins, drained)
- 600 ml vegetable stock (or water, if using dried lentils)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 carrots, finely diced
- 2 celery sticks, finely diced
- 400 g chestnut mushrooms, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 150 ml red wine (or extra stock)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp dried rosemary (or ½ tsp dried mixed herbs)
- 1 bay leaf
- Salt and black pepper
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley (or 1 tsp dried)

### For the mash topping

- 1 kg potatoes (Maris Piper or similar floury), peeled and chunked
- 100 ml milk
- 50 g butter
- Salt and black pepper
- 50 g grated cheddar (optional, for golden top)

## Method

1. **Cook the lentils (if using dried).** Rinse the lentils. Tip into a saucepan with the stock. Bring to the boil, reduce heat, and simmer for 20-25 minutes until tender but not mushy. Drain any excess liquid. (Skip this step if using tinned lentils — just drain them.)
2. **Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).**
3. **Make the filling base.** Heat the olive oil in a large deep frying pan or saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the onion, carrots, and celery. Cook for 8-10 minutes until softened and starting to colour.
4. **Add mushrooms.** Add the mushrooms and cook for 6-8 minutes until they've released their liquid and it's evaporated.
5. **Add aromatics.** Stir in the garlic, tomato purée, and flour. Cook for 2 minutes.
6. **Deglaze and simmer.** Pour in the red wine, let it bubble for 2-3 minutes until reduced. Add the soy sauce, thyme, rosemary, bay leaf, and the cooked lentils. Season with salt and pepper.
7. **Simmer for 10 minutes** until the mixture is thick and glossy. (If using tinned lentils, just heat through for 5 min.) Fish out the bay leaf. Stir in the parsley.
8. **Make the mash.** While the filling simmers, boil the potatoes in salted water for 15 minutes until tender. Drain well. Return to the pan, add the milk and butter, and mash until smooth. Season with salt and pepper.
9. **Assemble.** Tip the filling into a baking dish (about 20x30 cm). Spread the mash over the top, roughing it up with a fork for extra surface area (more crispy bits). Scatter with grated cheese if using.
10. **Bake for 25-30 minutes** until the top is golden and the filling is bubbling at the edges.
11. **Rest for 5-10 minutes** before serving (it'll be very hot — molten lava stage).

## Notes / variations

- **The lentils are doing 70% of the work.** Dried green or brown lentils hold their shape; red lentils go mushy. Use the right one.
- **The mash needs to be roughed up with a fork**, not smoothed flat. Those ridges and peaks = more crispy bits.
- **Make-ahead:** Assemble the pie (without baking), cover, and refrigerate for up to 2 days. Or freeze for up to 3 months. Bake from cold, adding 10-15 min to the cook time.
- **Make it vegan:** Use plant-based milk and butter in the mash, skip the cheese on top (or use vegan cheese).
- **Add more veg:** A handful of frozen peas in the filling, or some spinach wilted in.
- **The leftovers are better the next day.** Reheat in a 180°C oven until piping hot (about 20 min) — the microwave makes the topping go rubbery.
