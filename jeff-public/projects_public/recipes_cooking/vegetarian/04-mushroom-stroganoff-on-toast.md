# Mushroom Stroganoff on Toast (Vegetarian)

**Category:** vegetarian
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 5 min **Cook:** 25 min **Total:** 30 min

## Quick description

The 30-minute meat-free stroganoff. Loads of mushrooms (use a mix if you can — chestnut, portobello, even dried porcini), in a creamy paprika-dijon sauce, served on buttered toast. **The trick is getting the mushrooms properly browned — same rule as the creamy garlic mushroom fusilli. Don't crowd the pan.**

## Ingredients

- 600 g mixed mushrooms (chestnut, portobello, button — whatever's good), sliced
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 200 ml sour cream
- 100 ml vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp brandy or white wine (optional)
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley (or 1 tsp dried)
- Salt and black pepper
- 4 thick slices of bread (or 2 buns, halved)
- Butter, for the toast
- Lemon wedges, to serve

## Method

1. **Brown the mushrooms.** Heat the olive oil and half the butter in a large frying pan over **high heat**. Add the mushrooms in a single layer (work in batches — **don't crowd the pan**). Cook for 4-5 minutes without moving until deeply browned. Flip and brown the other side. Transfer to a plate.
2. **Sauté the aromatics.** Reduce heat to medium. Add the remaining butter to the pan. Add the onion and cook for 4-5 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and smoked paprika, cook for 1 minute.
3. **Deglaze.** Pour in the brandy (if using) and let it bubble for 30 seconds. Add the stock and Dijon mustard, stir to combine. Let it bubble for 2-3 minutes until slightly reduced.
4. **Add the cream and mushrooms.** Reduce heat to low. Stir in the sour cream (off the heat is important — boiling sour cream splits). Return the mushrooms to the pan. Stir to coat. Warm through for 1-2 minutes. Don't boil.
5. **Toast the bread.** Butter the bread generously. Toast until golden.
6. **Serve.** Pile the stroganoff onto the buttered toast. Scatter with parsley, a grind of pepper, and a lemon wedge on the side.

## Notes / variations

- **The mushrooms need to brown, not steam.** Wet mushrooms in a crowded pan = grey and rubbery. Dry mushrooms in a hot, roomy pan = golden, caramelised, deeply savoury. Don't skip the batch-cooking step.
- **The sour cream goes in off the heat** so it doesn't split. If it splits, whisk a tablespoon of cold water in vigorously.
- **Add depth:** A handful of dried porcini mushrooms, soaked in warm water for 10 min (use the soaking liquid as part of the stock) gives an extra layer of umami.
- **Make it a stroganoff pasta:** Toss with cooked pappardelle or fusilli instead of toast.
- **No brandy?** White wine, or just extra stock with a splash of lemon juice at the end.
- **The lemon is doing 20% of the work.** A squeeze of lemon at the end cuts through the richness and brightens the whole dish.

## Pairs with

- A simple green salad with a sharp vinaigrette
- A glass of white wine
- A side of pickles or cornichons
- The leftovers make a great pasta sauce the next day
