# Black Bean & Sweetcorn Quesadillas

**Category:** vegetarian
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 2 (4 halves)
**Prep:** 5 min **Cook:** 10 min **Total:** 15 min

## Quick description

The 15-minute Tex-Mex lunch. Tinned black beans, frozen sweetcorn, cheese, and spices, sandwiched between two tortillas and griddled until crispy. **The trick is toasting the outside of the tortilla until it's properly crisp and the cheese is fully melted — a sad, soft quesadilla is a crime.**

## Ingredients

- 4 flour tortillas
- 1 x 400 g tin black beans, drained and roughly mashed
- 200 g frozen sweetcorn (defrosted)
- 150 g grated cheese (cheddar, Monterey Jack, or a mix)
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 1 red pepper, finely diced
- 1 garlic clove, crushed
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp dried chilli flakes (optional)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper
- Sour cream or natural yoghurt, to serve
- Salsa or chopped tomatoes, to serve
- Fresh coriander or dried oregano, to finish
- Lime wedges, to serve

## Method

1. **Make the filling.** Heat the olive oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 4-5 minutes until soft. Add the red pepper, cook for 3-4 minutes. Add the garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, and chilli flakes, cook for 1 minute.
2. **Add the beans and corn.** Stir in the mashed black beans and the sweetcorn. Cook for 3-4 minutes until heated through and most of the liquid has evaporated. Season with salt and pepper. The mixture should be thick and not watery (watery filling = soggy quesadilla).
3. **Assemble the quesadillas.** Lay a tortilla flat. Spread half the bean mixture over one half of the tortilla. Sprinkle with half the cheese. Fold the empty half over to make a half-moon. Repeat with the other tortilla.
4. **Cook the quesadillas.** Wipe out the frying pan, return to medium heat. Add a teaspoon of olive oil. Lay a quesadilla in the pan. Cook for **2-3 minutes per side** until the tortilla is golden and crispy and the cheese is fully melted. Repeat with the second one.
5. **Serve.** Cut each quesadilla in half. Serve with sour cream, salsa, fresh coriander, and lime wedges.

## Notes / variations

- **Drain the beans properly.** Wet beans = soggy quesadilla. Drain, then mash.
- **The cheese is the glue.** Don't skimp. It needs to melt to hold the filling together when you cut it.
- **Want it meaty?** Add some cooked chorizo, bacon, or shredded chicken to the filling.
- **Make-ahead filling:** The bean mixture keeps in the fridge for 3-4 days. Use as a filling for tacos, burritos, or as a side dish.
- **Vegan version:** Skip the cheese (or use vegan cheese), use plant-based yoghurt.
- **Don't over-fill:** Too much filling and the quesadilla won't seal properly or the cheese can't hold it together.

## Pairs with

- Sour cream, salsa, guacamole
- A simple green salad
- A cold Mexican beer or a margarita
- Lime wedges (the acidity is essential)
