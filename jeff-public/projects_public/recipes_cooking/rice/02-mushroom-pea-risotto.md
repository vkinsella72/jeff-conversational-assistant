# Mushroom & Pea Risotto (No-Stir Method)

**Category:** rice
**Source:** Jeff's, 2026-07-22
**Created:** 2026-07-22
**Last made:** —
**Rating:** —
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 10 min **Cook:** 30 min **Total:** 40 min

## Quick description

The "I want risotto but I don't want to stand at the hob for 30 minutes stirring" risotto. **This is the no-stir oven method** — you still get the creamy, glossy texture without the constant attention. The trick is hot stock, oven heat, and a final vigorous stir with cold butter and parmesan at the end. **That final stir is what makes it risotto, not rice.**

## Ingredients

- 300 g risotto rice (arborio or carnaroli)
- 400 g chestnut mushrooms, sliced
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 150 ml white wine (or extra stock)
- 1 litre hot chicken or vegetable stock
- 200 g frozen peas
- 50 g butter (plus 30 g cold, for finishing)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 50 g grated parmesan (plus more to serve)
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- Salt and black pepper
- Fresh parsley or dried parsley, to finish

## Method

1. **Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan).**
2. **Brown the mushrooms.** Heat 1 tbsp olive oil and half the butter in a large oven-safe casserole or deep frying pan over high heat. Add the mushrooms in a single layer (work in batches if needed). Cook for 4-5 minutes without moving until deeply browned. Transfer to a plate.
3. **Sauté the aromatics.** Reduce heat to medium. Add the remaining olive oil. Add the onion and cook for 4-5 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and thyme, cook for 1 minute.
4. **Toast the rice.** Add the rice to the pan, stir to coat in the oil, and toast for 2 minutes until the grains are translucent at the edges.
5. **Deglaze.** Pour in the white wine, let it bubble for 2 minutes until mostly absorbed.
6. **Add stock and bake.** Pour in the hot stock (it should just cover the rice). Season with salt and pepper. Bring to a simmer on the hob, then transfer to the oven (covered with a lid or foil). Bake for **18-20 minutes**, until the rice is almost tender but still has a slight bite.
7. **Add peas and mushrooms.** Stir in the frozen peas and the browned mushrooms. Return to the oven for 2-3 minutes to heat through.
8. **The final stir (the risotto move).** Take the pan out of the oven. Add the cold butter and the parmesan. Stir vigorously for 1-2 minutes — this is what makes the rice creamy and glossy. The rice should be loose and flowing (not stiff), with a creamy sauce. If it's too thick, add a splash of hot stock; too thin, cook on the hob for another minute.
9. **Serve** immediately, with more parmesan and a scatter of parsley.

## Notes / variations

- **The final vigorous stir is the entire risotto move.** The cold butter + parmesan + the starch released by stirring = the creamy sauce. Don't skip it. Don't be gentle.
- **No oven?** Use the traditional method: add the stock a ladle at a time over 18-20 minutes, stirring frequently. Same result, more attention.
- **No wine?** Extra stock with a squeeze of lemon at the end.
- **Add protein:** Pan-fried chicken breast, crispy bacon, or cooked prawns stirred in at the end.
- **Vegan:** Skip the butter and parmesan. Use 100 ml coconut cream + 2 tbsp nutritional yeast stirred in at the end. Different vibe, still works.
- **Make ahead:** Risotto doesn't keep well — the rice absorbs the sauce and goes stiff. If you need to make it ahead, undercook by 2 minutes, then finish with the stir and extra stock when ready to serve.
